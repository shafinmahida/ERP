# AI CONTEXT — BUSINESS DOMAIN OVERVIEW

Dayar-E-Habib (Serving Pilgrims Since 1986) is an Indian travel operator managing Hajj and Umrah pilgrimages to Saudi Arabia (Makkah and Madinah).

## Core Domain Operations
1. **Pilgrim Registration**: Capturing customer profiles, passport identity documents, family relationships, emergency contacts.
2. **Season & Package Management**: Offering Hajj Deluxe, Hajj Standard, Umrah Executive, and Umrah Economy packages with fixed pricing and inclusions.
3. **Financial Billing & Ledger**: Generating human-friendly registration numbers (`DH-2026-HAJ-000248`), calculating net totals, collecting cash/cheque/bank payments, tracking balances, applying GST taxes.
4. **Travel Operations**: Submitting MOFA visa batches, managing airline carrier PNR tickets, allocating hotel rooming (Double/Triple/Quad sharing).
5. **Print Engine**: Printing A4 Combined Booking Forms, GST Tax Invoices, Receipts, ID Cards, and Barcode stickers.
