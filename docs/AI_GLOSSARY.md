# AI GLOSSARY — HAJJ & UMRAH DOMAIN TERMINOLOGY

- **PAX**: Passenger / Pilgrim. Represented by individual records in `registration_pax`.
- **Primary Pilgrim**: The head of the registration group responsible for primary contact and commercial billing.
- **Booking**: The commercial group registered together (e.g., family of 7).
- **Registration**: Individual enrollment in a specific trip/package/season (`DH-2026-HAJ-000248`).
- **MOFA**: Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs visa submission reference number.
- **PNR**: Passenger Name Record — 6-character airline reservation code.
- **Rooming Allocation**: Hotel room assignment (Quad, Triple, Double, Single sharing).
- **Immutability Snapshot**: Preserving historic package rates and names on registration records.
